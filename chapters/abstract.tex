\chapter*{ABSTRACT}
\onehalfspacing
   Gene expression data derived from RNA sequencing technologies typically consist of thousands of gene features measured on a relatively small number of biological samples.
    Such high-dimensional datasets present significant challenges for machine learning models, including overfitting, increased computational complexity, and reduced generalization performance. Feature selection techniques are commonly used to mitigate these challenges by identifying the most informative genes for classification tasks. However, the comparative impact of different feature selection methods on classification accuracy remains an open question in many high-dimensional biological datasets. This study aims to investigate how various feature selection approaches influence classification performance in bulk RNA-seq gene expression data. By applying filter, wrapper, and embedded feature selection methods and evaluating their effects using a consistent machine learning model, this research seeks to provide insight into dimensionality reduction strategies for improved predictive modeling in genomics.
